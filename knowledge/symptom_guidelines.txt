# Symptom Severity & Interpretation Guidelines

## Pain Level Interpretation
Scale: 0 to 10 numerical rating scale.

- 0–3 (Mild):
  - Description: Dull, noticeable, or periodic discomfort.
  - Functional Impact: Normal daily activities are usually uninterrupted.
  - Action: General home care, rest, or mild over-the-counter support as appropriate.

- 4–6 (Moderate):
  - Description: Persistent, noticeable pain or cramping.
  - Functional Impact: Some daily activities become difficult or require modification.
  - Action: Hydration, heat therapy, simple pain relief, and monitoring for pattern changes.

- 7–10 (Severe):
  - Description: Intense, sharp, disabling, or agonizing pain.
  - Functional Impact: Inability to perform basic daily activities, sleep, or move comfortably.
  - Action: Persistent or sudden severe pain warrants prompt evaluation by a healthcare provider or gynecologist.

## Flow Intensity Interpretation

- Light:
  - Description: Spotting, minimal pad/tampon usage (1–2 light products/day), or lasting under 2 days.
  - Context: Common with hormonal contraception, perimenopause, or early pregnancy; investigate if sudden or persistent.

- Moderate:
  - Description: Soaking 3–5 regular pads/tampons per day, lasting 3–7 days.
  - Context: Normal physiological range for most individuals.

- Heavy:
  - Description: Soaking a high-absorbency pad/tampon every 1–2 hours for consecutive hours, passing large clots (>2.5 cm).
  - Context: High risk for iron deficiency; requires medical evaluation if routine or accompanied by fatigue.

---

## Stress Impact Interpretation

- Low Impact:
  - Description: Occasional daily stressors, temporary feeling of pressure.
  - Hormonal Effect: Unlikely to alter cycle timing or flow.

- Moderate Impact:
  - Description: Prolonged stress over weeks, noticeable changes in sleep or mood.
  - Hormonal Effect: May cause minor cycle shifts (2–5 days late/early) or heightened PMS symptoms.

- High Impact:
  - Description: Chronic high anxiety, severe life events, ongoing burnout.
  - Hormonal Effect: High cortisol levels can delay or suppress ovulation, leading to missed periods (amenorrhea) or irregular spotting.

---

## Sleep Quality Interpretation

- Optimal (7–9 hours continuous):
  - Endocrine Impact: Supports baseline LH, FSH, and cortisol rhythm regulation.

- Moderately Disrupted (5–6 hours or frequent awakenings):
  - Endocrine Impact: Mild circadian disruption; can worsen mood swings, fatigue, and pain sensitivity during PMS.

- Severely Disrupted (<5 hours or chronic insomnia):
  - Endocrine Impact: Significant disruption to reproductive hormone secretion, elevated cortisol, potential cycle irregularity, and lowered pain threshold.

---

## Cycle Length Deviation

- Normal (21–35 days):
  - Regular baseline cycle length.

- Mild Variance (Variation of 3–7 days month-to-month):
  - Often related to acute stress, travel, illness, or diet changes; usually self-resolving.

- Significant Variance (<21 days, >35 days, or missing 3+ consecutive cycles):
  - Suggests potential underlying hormonal imbalance (e.g., PCOS, thyroid disorder, hypothalamic amenorrhea) requiring clinical follow-up.

---

## PCOS Symptom Clusters

- Low Likelihood Cluster:
  - Single isolated symptom (e.g., mild acne alone with fully regular cycles).

- Moderate Likelihood Cluster:
  - Irregular cycles combined with one metabolic or dermatological sign (e.g., persistent acne or mild hirsutism).

- High Likelihood Cluster:
  - Classic triad or strong combination: Irregular/absent periods + clinical signs of excess androgens (hirsutism, male-pattern hair thinning, severe acne) + metabolic indicators (weight gain resistance, acanthosis nigricans).
  - Recommendation: Comprehensive evaluation including pelvic ultrasound and androgen blood panels.

---

## Combined Symptom Patterns (Triage Logic)

- Pattern A (Severe Pain + Heavy Flow):
  - Clinical Note: Classic presentation for conditions like endometriosis, adenomyosis, or uterine fibroids.
  - Action: Gynecological consultation recommended, especially if accompanied by signs of anemia (dizziness, severe fatigue).

- Pattern B (Missed Period + Severe Unilateral Pelvic Pain):
  - Clinical Note: Red-flag pattern; potential sign of an ectopic pregnancy or ruptured ovarian cyst.
  - Action: Immediate urgent medical evaluation required.

- Pattern C (Irregular Cycles + High Stress + Sleep Disruption):
  - Clinical Note: Functional hypothalamic disturbance or HPA-axis dysregulation.
  - Action: Lifestyle, sleep hygiene, and stress mitigation, alongside baseline hormonal blood work if persistent.
